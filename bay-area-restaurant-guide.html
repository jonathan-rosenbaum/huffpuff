A Curated Dining Guide

Seven Gems of the
East Bay

A personal guide to our favorite restaurants across Oakland, Berkeley & Alameda

Explore the Guide

The East Bay is home to one of the most vibrant and diverse dining scenes in California. From waterfront Thai to wood-fired seafood, Burmese street food to Pan-American grill — these seven restaurants each tell a story worth savoring.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone, enjoying a quiet lunch, or sharing a meal with someone you love, this guide is for you.

01
Thai · Jack London Square

Farmhouse Kitchen
Thai Cuisine

336 Water Street, Oakland

★★★★☆ 4.5 · Google & OpenTable

“Thai New Generation” cuisine by Chef Kasem “Pop” Saengsawang, who won Chopped Season 61. Rooted in his childhood in Loei, Thailand, this Michelin Bib Gourmand kitchen delivers bold, uncompromised flavors with vibrant waterfront energy.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Hat Yai Fried Chicken
  • Khao Soi
  • Panang Nuea Short Ribs
  • Bangkok Herbal Rice Salad
  • Thai Kabocha Fries
Price $20–30 per person
Phone (510) 419-0541
Hours Mon–Fri 11:30a–8:30p
Sat–Sun 12p–9p
Website farmhousethai.com
02
Japanese · Fourth Street

iyasare

1830 Fourth Street, Berkeley

★★★★☆ 4.5 · Michelin Guide Listed

A serene izakaya where Japanese tradition meets California seasonality. Chef Shotaro Kamio crafts exquisite small plates using local ingredients, served in a minimalist space that feels like a quiet exhale. The name means “to be healed” in Japanese.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Seasonal Omakase
  • Japanese Curry
  • Grilled Rice Ball
  • Sake Flight
  • Karaage
Price $30–50 per person
Neighborhood Fourth Street, Berkeley
Hours Tue–Sun 5p–9p
Mon Closed
Website iyasare-berkeley.com
03
North Indian · Alameda

India Palace

737 Buena Vista Avenue, Alameda

★★★★☆ 4.3 · #1 Indian on Yelp (Alameda)

A beloved island institution since 1991, women-owned India Palace is nestled inside a charming renovated house. The tandoor runs hot, the lunch buffet is legendary, and the heated patio may be the finest outdoor dining spot in Alameda. BYO wine welcome.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Lunch Buffet
  • Tandoori Lamb Chops
  • Navrattan Korma
  • Malai Kofta
  • Mango Kulfi
Price ~$30 per person
Phone (510) 814-8778
Hours Mon–Sat 11a–2p, 5p–9:30p
Sun dinner only
Website alamedaindiapalace.com
04
Burmese · Temescal

Burma Superstar

4721 Telegraph Avenue, Oakland

★★★★☆ 4.0–4.5 · Bay Area Institution

The restaurant that put Burmese cuisine on the Bay Area map. Known for jaw-dropping lines and a tea leaf salad that has its own cult following, Burma Superstar bridges the cuisines of Myanmar, India, and Southeast Asia in every dish.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Tea Leaf Salad
  • Rainbow Salad
  • Fiery Tofu
  • Chili Lamb
  • Coconut Chicken Noodle Soup
Price $15–25 per person
Neighborhood Temescal, Oakland
Hours Sun–Thu 11:30a–9p
Fri–Sat 11:30a–9:30p
Website burmasuperstar.co
05
Pan-American · Downtown

Bocanova

1111 Broadway, Oakland

★★★★☆ 4.6 · TripAdvisor

Latinx and women-owned, Bocanova celebrates the cuisine of the Americas with a California heart. Chef Rick Hackett (Chez Panisse) and co-chef Martin Ladera (Peru & Japan) craft a seasonal menu around raw bar, rotisserie, and wood-fired plancha.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Ceviche Verde Tostada
  • Peruvian Chicken a la Brasa
  • Dungeness Crab Deviled Eggs
  • Pork Chop
  • Sunday Supper Prix Fixe
Price $25–40 per person
Phone (510) 497-1111
Hours Mon–Fri 11:30a–8:30p
Sat 12p–8p · Sun 12p–7p
Website bocanovaoakland.com
06
Seafood · Grand Lake

Sirene

3308 Grand Avenue, Oakland

★★★★★ 4.7 · Google

A live-fire seafood bistro on charming Grand Avenue. Sirene combines whole-animal and sustainable sourcing with the primal allure of wood-fired cooking. The intimate space glows with warmth — the kind of neighborhood gem that makes you feel like a regular on your first visit.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Whole Grilled Fish
  • Wood-Fired Oysters
  • Grilled Octopus
  • Seasonal Crudo
  • House-Made Pasta
Price $35–55 per person
Neighborhood Grand Lake, Oakland
Hours Wed–Sun 5p–9:30p
Mon–Tue Closed
Website sirene-oak.com
07
Waterfront Seafood · Berkeley Marina

Skates on the Bay

100 Seawall Drive, Berkeley

★★★★☆ 4.5 · OpenTable Diners’ Choice

Open since 1984, Skates offers perhaps the most breathtaking dining view in the East Bay — panoramic windows on three sides frame the Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco skyline, and sunset over the water. Come for the view, stay for the Dungeness crab.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Dungeness Crab Linguini
  • Herb Crusted Prime Rib
  • Clam Chowder
  • Kung Pao Calamari
  • Key Lime Pie
Price $40–75 per person
Phone (510) 549-1900
Hours Daily 11:30a–9p
Happy Hour Mon–Thu 3p–5p
Website skatesonthebay.com
